# Fukui Prefecture Long-Term Vision - Open Data

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

This project provides the [Fukui Prefecture Long-Term Vision](https://www.pref.fukui.lg.jp/doc/seiki/vision2019/top.html) as structured, accessible open data. The content is also available as a [mobile-friendly website](https://code4fukui.github.io/vision/).

We are continually updating the content with new data and illustrations to make it easier to understand. Contributions are welcome!

## Vision Outline

### Future Concept (将来構想)

![Fukui Prefecture's Envisioned Future](https://code4fukui.github.io/vision/img/fukuivision2.jpg)


-   **[Basic Principles (基本理念.md)](基本理念.md)**
    -   "Connecting 'Fukui of Peace of Mind' to the future, with more challenges and more fun!"
-   **[2040 Vision for Fukui (2040年の福井県の将来像.md)](2040年の福井県の将来像.md)**
    1.  Fukui of Confidence and Pride
    2.  Fukui where Everyone is a Star
    3.  Fukui Taking a Great Leap
-   **[Characteristics of Fukui (福井県の特徴.md)](福井県の特徴.md)**
    1.  Richness of nature and food
    2.  Strong family and community ties
    3.  Excellent child-rearing and educational environment
    4.  High-quality human resources
    5.  Stable industrial and employment base
-   **Global Trends and Environmental Changes**
